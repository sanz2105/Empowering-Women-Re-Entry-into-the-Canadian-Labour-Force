## 💡 Project Overview

This repository contains the code, analysis, and visualizations for the project **"Empowering Women: Identifying Barriers and Opportunities for Re-entry into the Canadian Labour Force"**, developed using the **Labour Force Survey (LFS) Dataset**.

Canadian women often exit the workforce due to caregiving or life transitions and face challenges in re-entering. This project leverages data science to uncover **barriers** and identify **data-driven opportunities** to inform inclusive policy interventions.

---

## 📊 Dataset

- **Source:** Canadian Labour Force Survey (LFS)
- **Format:** CSV / Excel
- **Scope:** Demographics, education, employment status, marital status, weekly work hours, wages, and more.

---

## 🔍 Goals & Objectives

- Identify **key socioeconomic barriers** preventing women from rejoining the workforce.
- Explore **employment patterns**, **education levels**, and **family structure influences**.
- Provide **policy recommendations** for more inclusive labor force participation.
- Use **EDA** and **interactive visualizations** to effectively communicate findings.

## 🧩 Key Findings

- Women are **overrepresented** in the "Not in labour force" category.
- Women show **higher educational attainment**, yet lower employability.
- **Marital status**, **family responsibilities**, and **part-time work** create structural barriers.
- **Single and divorced women** are especially vulnerable in labor market re-entry.

## 🎯 Recommendations
